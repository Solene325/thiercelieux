- `thiercelieux-logo-transparent.webp` : signature complète sur fond transparent, pour l’accueil.
- `thiercelieux-emblem-transparent.webp` : emblème transparent du même logo, pour les en-têtes.

Les versions avec fond sont conservées sous `thiercelieux-logo.webp` et `thiercelieux-emblem.webp`. Le logo d’origine est conservé dans les images générées de la session.

## Prompt de génération

> Create a beautiful, distinctive premium application logo for a French werewolf social deduction game named THIERCELIEUX. Deliver a single finished logo proposal, centered on a perfectly uniform deep midnight navy background #0d1420, square composition with generous clean margins. This is the actual logo artwork, NOT a website screenshot, NOT a product mockup, NOT a poster. Main emblem: a masterfully sculpted realistic wolf head in three-quarter view, alert and intelligent rather than snarling, made of finely engraved dark silver with restrained aged gold edges. A slender antique brass crescent gracefully frames the back of its head, integrated into a compact coherent silhouette. Tiny architectural cuts at the base subtly evoke the pointed roofs of an old French village, without becoming an illustrated landscape. Strong recognizable silhouette, elegant negative spaces, balanced proportions; detailed realistic fur relief and credible metal texture at close range, yet immediately readable as a logo when small. The wolf's eye is natural amber with no supernatural glow. Restrained studio lighting, soft shadows, refined craftsmanship like an exceptional antique seal. Below the emblem, beautifully spaced, perfectly legible custom Roman serif lettering in warm pale gold: exactly 'THIERCELIEUX'. A much smaller carefully spaced subtitle below: exactly 'LES LOUPS-GAROUS'. The lettering should feel designed and harmonize with the emblem, not a generic fantasy font. Palette limited to midnight navy, charcoal silver, aged brass and pale gold. Avoid excessive ornament, kitsch fantasy badges, angry esports mascots, plastic CGI, flaming eyes, glowing neon, lens flares, heavy bloom, elaborate scenery, photorealistic animal photography, extra symbols, any extra text or watermark. Aim for a sophisticated, original, realistic and memorable game identity.

## Prompt de détourage

> Background removal only. This is an APPROVED logo; do not redesign or reinterpret it. Remove the uniform midnight navy background and replace it with true transparent alpha, including the empty gaps around the crescent, fur and village and the spaces between and inside the letters. Preserve the exact wolf, every strand of its silver fur, amber eye, antique gold crescent and tiny village buildings, their original position and appearance, every letter of the wordmark 'THIERCELIEUX' and subtitle 'LES LOUPS-GAROUS', gold texturing and serif shapes, original composition and margins. Keep the dark shading INSIDE the solid wolf and village opaque; do not make the animal translucent. Do not add anything or crop any lettering. Deliver the same approved logo on a genuinely transparent square canvas, preserving the original placement proportions, so it can be placed cleanly over a landscape in the app.
